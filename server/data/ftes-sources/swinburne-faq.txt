Shaping the Future: Swinburne Sarawak Supports the Sarawak Free Tertiary Education Scheme
The Sarawak Government’s Free Tertiary Education Scheme (FTES), set to be implemented in 2026, is a landmark initiative aimed at expanding access to quality higher education for eligible Sarawakian students.
As a participating university, Swinburne University of Technology Sarawak Campus affirms its support for the scheme’s implementation and is committed to facilitating student awareness and preparedness as further details become available from the relevant government authorities.
This webpage serves as a central information hub and will be updated periodically with information on:
- Eligibility criteria and funding scope
- Application and admission processes
- Swinburne Sarawak programs approved under the scheme
- FAQs, and relevant information
Our Future Students Team is available to provide relevant guidance on program offerings, potential career pathways, and preparations for the 2026 intake under the FTES.

FAQs for Sarawak Government's Initiative of Free Higher Education
1. Do I need to apply for FTES?
Yes, Eligible students who wish to be considered for FTES are required to submit their applications online.
2. When will I receive the application outcome?
The outcome of your application will be communicated to you via your official Swinburne student email account once it is available.
3. Do I need to continue paying my tuition fees for 2026 while waiting for the application outcome?
Eligible Students who have applied for FTES do not need to pay their tuition fees for 2026 while waiting for the application outcome.
Treasury Services would be making relevant fee adjustments after the census date (Week 4).
We suggest to check your student statement via Student Portal after Week 4 of your study for an accurate reflection of your account.
4. I failed a unit. Is this covered under FTES?
Any extension or additional fees due to a failed or repeated unit will not be funded and must be borne solely by the student.
5. I am currently receiving/holding a scholarship/education financial aid intended to cover the tuition fees for my program. Am I eligible to apply for FTES?
Yes, you may submit an application. However, you will need to cancel your current scholarship/education aid once your application is approved.
6. When will the Sarawak Government’s Free Higher Education Initiative begin?
The Premier of Sarawak has announced that the initiative will commence in 2026.
7. Can I study at Swinburne Sarawak under the Sarawak Government's Free Higher Education Initiative from 2026?
Yes, starting in 2026 and if you are an eligible Sarawakian, you will have the opportunity to pursue your studies at Swinburne Sarawak under this initiative.
For Sarawakian only.

12. If my child enrolls now (in 2025), will they benefit from the Free Higher Education Initiative in 2026?
Yes, any student who meets the eligibility criterias and applies for the approved programs under FTES will benefit from the Free Tertiary Education Scheme from 2026 onwards.
13. What are the requirements to study under the Free Higher Education Initiative at Swinburne Sarawak in 2026?
All Sarawakians with a 'K' in their IC or Malaysians with a Sarawakian parent (with 'K' in their IC) are eligible for the Free Tertiary Education Scheme (FTES) for their first undergraduate degree, provided that they are not receiving any other educational sponsorship or scholarships. Additionally, they may accept FTES only once and only from one institution.
14. Is there any financial support available before 2026 for students at Swinburne Sarawak?
Yes. Swinburne Sarawak offers a range of bursaries and scholarships to support students financially.
Additionally, Yayasan Sarawak provides financial aid, including study loans and scholarships. For more details, please visit Yayasan Sarawak’s official website.

19. Is Bahasa Melayu required as part of the entry requirement for FTES?
No. However, the relevant MQA requirements will still be applicable for completion of degree at Swinburne Sarawak.
20. What's the process and documents needed to apply for stipend?
Students are required to submit official supporting documentations, such as certified true copies of payslips endorsed by commissioner of oaths, authorised management officers, or verification by Ketua Masyarakat dan Ketua Kaum (KMKK).
21. Will FTES cover fees during exchange? If yes, is there any quota specifically for FTES students to apply for exchange?
FTES will only cover tuition fees for programs delivered locally. However, students are still welcome to apply for Exchange Programs at their own cost.
22. Is there a minimum CGPA that students must maintain each semester to remain eligible for FTES?
Students are required to pass every unit in their degree program. Any extension or additional fees due to a failed or repeated unit will not be funded and must be borne solely by the student.
23. Are current Swinburne students eligible to apply for FTES? If yes, will fees cover their entire program or just the remaining duration?
Yes. FTES will include fees for the remaining duration of their degrees.
24. How long will students receive the Eligibilty Letter for FTES upon application.
Students will receive their eligibility letters together with their offer letters for the degree.
25. Is MPU fee covered under FTES?
Yes.
26. Are students required to pay RM500 deposit upon enrolment to Swinburne? What about those who has paid earlier? Will this be refunded?
No, students under FTES will not be required to pay the RM 500 deposit.
27. Does failing a semester affect the stipend disbursement?
No.
28. Are students with IGCSE qualifications eligible to apply for FTES?
FTES only applies to Degree programs. Students using IGCSE or equivalent qualifications may apply to Foundation or Diploma programs.
29. Are students required to submit their academic transcripts to MIETD/YS every semester?
No. This will be managed by the University.
30. Are students allowed to go on transfer when they are under FTES?
FTES will only cover tuition fees for programs delivered locally. Tuition fees once a student transfers to Australia will not be covered.
31. If a student studies overseas and comes back to Swinburne to apply for a degree, will FTES still refer back to SPM to meet eligibility?
Eligibility for degree programmes under FTES will be assessed based on the applicant’s highest qualification and the existing entry requirements for the programme. For more information, please refer to the academic entry requirements.
32. Can a former Swinburne student who withdrew or LOA with incomplete degree, eligible for ftes if they come back to Swinburne?
Yes.
33. If students half way quits the degree, will they be required to pay back fees under FTES?
Students who quit their program halfway will be not required to re pay any frees covered by FTES.

35. What should I do if I am currently receiving another scholarship or sponsorship and wish to switch to FTES?
Students who are currently receiving other scholarships or sponsorships and intend to switch to FTES must first terminate their existing sponsorship or loan, as FTES recipients are not allowed to hold or receive any other scholarship or financial aid intended to cover the tuition fees for the same programme.
For students on Yayasan Sarawak Loan:
As per the existing Loan Agreement, the process follows the standard procedure for switching to another sponsorship body. Borrowers are required to:
- Terminate the existing loan; and
- Repay all loan monies disbursed to date in full (no discounts or incentives apply).
Borrowers may submit an appeal for Deferment of Repayment, allowing the repayment schedule to commence after completion of the Bachelor’s degree, subject to approval.
To proceed with loan termination, borrowers must submit the following:
- New sponsorship offer letter (e.g. FTES Offer Letter or official confirmation that studies are covered under FTES)
- Appeal for Deferment of Repayment (formal email or letter, if applicable)
All documents must be emailed to:
With the following recipients copied (CC):
- Loan Disbursement Section
- Loan Repayment Section
- Student Financial Advisory, Swinburne Sarawak
For PTPTN recipients:
Students are required to visit the PTPTN office counter at Jalan Kulas to request loan termination and follow the subsequent instructions provided by PTPTN.
For all other scholarship or sponsorship recipients:
Students may contact Student Financial Advisory at
or visit Student HQ, Counter 11.
Eligibility
All Sarawakians with a 'K' in their IC or Malaysians with a Sarawakian parent (with 'K' in their IC) are eligible for the Free Tertiary Education Scheme (FTES) for their first undergraduate degree, provided that they are not receiving any other educational sponsorship or scholarships. Additionally, they may accept FTES only once and only from one institution.
Eligibility for Stipend
Eligible students: From households with a monthly per capita income of RM1,500 or below.
Applicable to: Students pursuing their first undergraduate degree at one of the four state-owned institutions of higher learning without support from any other educational sponsorship or scholarship.
Program: Approved under the Sarawak Free Tertiary Education Scheme (FTES).
Support Provided: Eligible students will be entitled to:
- RM10,000 annual stipend to assist with living expenses, including but not limited to food, transportation, and ancillary study needs.
- RM5,000 for accommodation through the designated university housing unit.
