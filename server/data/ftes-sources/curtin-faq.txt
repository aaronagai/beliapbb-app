Got questions about Free Tertiary Education Scheme (FTES)?
What is Free Tertiary Education Scheme (FTES)?
FTES is a Sarawak Government initiative for higher education in Sarawak which involves financial aid covering tuition fees for eligible students, commencing in 2026.
When is the start date for the Sarawak Government’s FTES?
The initiative has officially launched and will take full effect in 2026.
What are the objectives of the FTES?
The FTES aims to:
- Provide equal access to higher education
- Develop a highly skilled and educated workforce
- Reduce financial constraints on students and families
- Encourage continuous learning and academic excellence
Will the FTES cover study at Curtin Malaysia from 2026 onwards?
Yes. Eligible Sarawakians will be able to enrol in selected bachelor’s programs at Curtin Malaysia under the scheme starting in 2026.
Am I eligible to apply for the FTES in 2026?
To qualify for FTES while studying at Curtin Malaysia, students must fulfil the following criteria:
- Anak Sarawak;
- first bachelor degree;
- offered into an FTES-approved bachelor program under Curtin Malaysia;
- a full-time student; and
- not receiving any other scholarship.*
Students need to indicate their intention to apply for FTES when applying for admission into an approved bachelor degree program at Curtin Malaysia.
* Students currently under a scholarship must submit a scholarship cancellation letter from the awarding organisation to Curtin Malaysia if they wish to apply for FTES.
I am currently holding a scholarship/loan. Am I eligible for FTES and what is the process to opt for FTES?
If you are currently holding any scholarship or education loan, you are not eligible for FTES.
However, if you wish to opt for FTES, you may apply for FTES from eStudent > My Scholarship latest by 16 January 2026.
You must contact your respective sponsors to terminate your current scholarship/loan should you are deemed eligible for FTES. An Eligibility Letter will be issued to all eligible students.
- YS BP40
For YS BP40 recipients, please contact the Loans & Scholarships Office at loans.scholarships@curtin.edu.my to initiate withdrawal from the YS BP40 scheme, subject to the BP40 terms and conditions.
- Yayasan Sarawak Loan
Students under the Yayasan Sarawak Loan are required to contact the Repayment Section directly via ys@yayasansarawak.org.my or contribution@yayasansarawak.org.my to enquire for cancellation or termination in accordance with the loan’s terms and conditions. Please ensure that Loans and Scholarships Office is copied in the email.
- PTPTN Loan
For PTPTN loan cancellation, please visit the nearest PTPTN branch to obtain guidance on the cancellation procedure. The list of PTPTN branches is available at:
https://www.ptptn.gov.my/pejabat-cawangan-ptptn/
- Other Scholarship
For other forms of scholarships, please liaise directly with your respective sponsors. Please note that you may be required to fulfil any breach obligations with the sponsors.

Does the FTES cover only tuition fees?
In addition to tuition fees coverage, FTES also offers a RM15,000 stipend per annum for eligible students. This stipend assists with living expenses such ad housing, food, transport and other study needs. It is available to students whose household per capita income is RM1,500 per month or below, verified through the required official documentation.
Additional fees, if any, are to be covered by the student.
Do I need to pay for MPU fees?
No, you will not be required to pay for MPU units as they are covered under FTES.
Does the FTES have any age limit?
As of now, there is no age limit until further guidelines are issued by the Sarawak Government.
How do I apply for the FTES as a new student who wants to study at Curtin Malaysia?
- Submit your FTES-approved course application to Curtin Malaysia. The university will review your documents and check if you meet the eligibility requirements for FTES
- If you qualify, Curtin Malaysia will issue both your course Offer Letter and FTES Eligibility Letter
- Once you accept the offer, the university will finalise your admission and provide an Award Letter for the scheme before you enrol.
Are current students eligible to apply for the FTES in 2026?
Yes, current full-time students will be eligible for the initiative as long as their course is included in the approved list.
That means if the students start their bachelor’s at Curtin Malaysia in 2025 and continues full-time into 2026, they will receive full tuition coverage once the initiative begins.
Kindly contact our Loans & Scholarships Team at loans.scholarships@curtin.edu.my for more information on how to apply as a current student.
How do I apply for the FTES as a current student at Curtin Malaysia?
- If you are already enrolled in an FTES-approved course at Curtin Malaysia, the university will issue you an Eligibility Letter
- You’ll then confirm whether you want to opt in to the scheme and notify the university of your decision
- Once you accept, Curtin Malaysia will issue your Award Letter before you continue your studies under the scheme.
What happens if a student changes, defers, transfers or faces any interruption in their studies under the FTES?
Under FTES, students may change their program once within the same Sarawak-owned Institutions of Higher Learning (SIHLs) (such as Curtin Malaysia) subject to the Ministry’s approval. Transfers between SIHLs are not permitted. Bachelor’s degree students from other Institutions of Higher Learning who wish to enrol at Curtin Malaysia under FTES must submit a new application and, if approved by the Ministry, will be required to start their program from Year 1.
Students may defer their studies for valid reasons for up to two (2) semesters with approval from Curtin Malaysia and the implementing agency, during which FTES support will be suspended. FTES does not cover any extensions or additional fees due to failed units or repeated papers. If a student is suspended, terminated, or withdrawn, FTES support will also be revoked.
What can a student do if their FTES application is rejected?
If an application is rejected, the student has the right to appeal in writing through the implementing agency within fourteen (14) calendar days from the date of official notification, stating the grounds and providing supporting documents. The Minister will review each appeal on a case-by-case basis. The Minister’s decision shall be final, binding, and not subject to further review.
Will students under the FTES still be eligible for the Campus Transfer Program?
The initiative covers tuition fees for locally delivered programs only. Should a student transfer to Australia, tuition fees will no longer be covered under this initiative.
