SARAWAK
FREE
TERTIARY
EDUCATION
SCHEME
(FTES)
The Right Honourable Premier of Sarawak has introduced the
Sarawak Free Tertiary Education Scheme (FTES) in Sarawak
Budget 2023 to enable Anak Sarawak to pursue higher
education at Sarawak-owned institutions of higher learning
(SIHL), starting in 2026.
The Scheme not only covers tuition fees but also provides a
monthly stipend to eligible students to support their basic
living expenses throughout the duration of their studies.
The Scheme focuses on STEM, Medicine, Law, Accounting,
Finance and Psychology undergraduate degree programmes
to align with Sarawak’s talent development needs.
Sarawak FTES is another initiative by the Sarawak
Government to strengthen the education sector and support
Sarawak’s aspiration of becoming a developed state by 2030.

APPLICATION
1
3
CHANGE OF PROGRAMME
Students may change their programme once within the same
SIHL, with Ministry approval. Additional fees, if any, are to be
covered by the student.
RESPONSIBILITY OF BENEFICIARIES
2
Students must follow all university rules and complete their
degree within the period stated in the offer letter. The Scheme
only covers this period. Any extension or additional costs will
be the student’s own responsibility.
WHAT YOU SHOULD
KNOW
11
SARAWAK FREE TERTIARY EDUCATION SCHEME
CHANGE OF UNIVERSITY
4
Students under FTES are not permitted to transfer between
SIHLs.
Bachelor’s degree students from other institutions who wish
to join a SIHL under the FTES must submit a new application.
If approved, they will start their studies from Year 1.
SUSPENSION, TERMINATION, WITHDRAWAL
7
FTES, including stipend, will be suspended or terminated if
the student has been suspended, terminated and withdrawn
from or by the University.
EXTENSION
6
Any extension or additional fees due to failed or repeated
subjects will not be funded and must be borne solely by the
student.
DEFERMENT
5
Deferment is allowed for up to 2 semesters for valid reasons.
FTES will be suspended during this period.
