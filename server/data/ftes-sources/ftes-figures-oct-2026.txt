Title: FTES enrolment figures as of 30 September 2026 (DUN winding-up speech)
Source: Report by The Borneo Post, 19 October 2026, of the winding-up speech by Education, Innovation and Talent Development Minister Dato Sri Roland Sagah Wee Inn at the Sarawak State Legislative Assembly (DUN) sitting in Kuching. These are the latest official FTES figures.

- FTES intake for 2026 across the 64 covered programmes was 3,491 students, up 128.5% (1,963 more) from 1,528 in 2025.
- As of 30 September 2026, 6,246 students had accepted FTES offers at the Sarawak-owned institutions of higher learning (SIHL).
- Students who accepted FTES offers, by institution: Swinburne University of Technology Sarawak Campus 2,802; University of Technology Sarawak (UTS) 2,031; Curtin University Malaysia 949; i-CATS University College 464.
- 3,195 of the students who accepted offers qualified for the FTES stipend of RM15,000 a year (for students from lower-income households, monthly household income per capita RM1,500 or less), covering living and accommodation costs.
- The 64 programmes are aligned with Sarawak's strategic economic priorities, including the hydrogen economy, precision agriculture, data centre development and the aerospace industry.
- The ministry runs outreach programmes about FTES and other higher education opportunities, especially in rural communities.
- Earlier figures: 4,694 students as of 31 March 2026, and 4,837 acceptances as of May 2026. The Sarawak Government allocated RM250 million for FTES in 2026 and aimed for about 10,000 students in 2026.
