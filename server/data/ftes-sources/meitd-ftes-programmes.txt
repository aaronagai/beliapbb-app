Title: List of approved degree programmes under FTES (Senarai program ijazah yang diluluskan di bawah FTES)
Publisher: Ministry of Education, Innovation and Talent Development Sarawak (MEITD) — FTES booklet linked by QR code from the official FTES FAQ
Date: June 2026; checked against the MEITD FAQ page updated 29 October 2026

FTES covers 64 approved undergraduate (Bachelor's / Ijazah Sarjana Muda) programmes at the four Sarawak-owned institutions of higher learning (SIHL): Swinburne Sarawak 22, Curtin Malaysia 20, UTS 16, i-CATS 6. Programmes not on this list are not covered by FTES.
Fields: STEM (science, technology, engineering and mathematics), accounting, finance and psychology. Medicine and Law are in scope but are only to be offered under FTES once available at an SIHL; as of this list no Medicine or Law programme is offered. Perubatan dan Undang-undang akan ditawarkan apabila tersedia di SIHL.
Each programme below is listed with its university. Program yang disenaraikan di bawah ditawarkan percuma (yuran pengajian) di bawah FTES.
