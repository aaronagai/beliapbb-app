Title: 4,694 students enrolled under FTES for Q1 2026
Publisher: The Borneo Post
Date: 19 May 2026
Source: https://www.theborneopost.com/2026/05/19/4694-students-enrolled-under-ftes-for-1q-2026/

KUCHING (May 19): The Sarawak Free Tertiary Education Scheme (FTES) has successfully extended educational access to 4,694 eligible students who have accepted offers as at the first quarter of 2026.
Minister for Education, Innovation and Talent Development Dato Sri Roland Sagah Wee Inn said the cohort comprises 1,834 newly enrolled students and 2,860 continuing students under the initiative.
“Of these, 75 per cent are enrolled in Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics (STEM) courses, while the remaining 25 per cent are pursuing fields such as accounting, finance, and psychology,” he said in his winding-up speech.
He added that enrolment across state-owned institutions comprised Curtin University Malaysia with 543 students, i-CATS University College with 172 students, Swinburne University of Technology Sarawak with 1,959 students, and University of Technology Sarawak with 2,020 students.
“It is important to note that every course offered under this scheme is fully certified by the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA), ensuring that we do not compromise on the quality or global recognition of the qualifications earned,” he said.
While the scheme remained robust, Sagah noted that 1,344 individuals have opted to decline FTES, mainly in favour of alternative scholarships or external funding opportunities.
He also stressed that the Sarawak government remained committed to ensuring student welfare, acknowledging that daily living costs can often hinder academic success.
“Therefore, I am proud to report that 2,893 students, or 61.63 per cent of all FTES beneficiaries, have qualified for a stipend of RM10,000 for living expenses and RM5,000 for accommodation expenses,” he added.
On proposals to extend FTES to public universities for diploma courses, Sagah said a comprehensive study on the scheme’s effectiveness and impact is required before any decision can be made.
“Additionally, the disbursement of the stipend is in progress, subject to verification of applicants’ documentation,” he said in response to Chong Chieng Jen (DAP-Padungan), who raised the matter.
